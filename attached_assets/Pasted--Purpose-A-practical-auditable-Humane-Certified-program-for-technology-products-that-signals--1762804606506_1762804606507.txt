### Purpose

A practical, auditable "Humane Certified" program for technology products that signals user well‑being, data respect, transparency, and ecosystem impact.

---

### 1) Name and positioning

- Shortlist: Humane Certified • Humane-Ready • Trustworthy Tech Certified • HumaneBench Certified • Certified for Human Benefit
- Tagline ideas: "Built for people, proven in practice." "Independent, auditable standards for humane tech."
- Initial scope: software and AI products; expand to hardware later.

---

### 2) Landscape scan (adjacent programs)

- Security/compliance: SOC 2, ISO 27001, FedRAMP
- Privacy/AI: GDPR, ISO 42001 (AI MS), NIST AI RMF, model cards
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2
- Ethics/safety: OECD AI, Partnership on AI guidance, safety evals
- Sustainability: device energy, carbon disclosures
- Gap we fill: a consumer- and buyer-friendly trust mark tying product experience harms + data governance + AI safety into one auditable label.

---

### 3) Criteria v1.0 (auditable and outcome‑oriented)

Each check is labeled Self‑attest / Evidence required / Audit required. “Audit” items are sampled by an independent reviewer.

- Product experience and harms
    - Harm triage and red‑team logs exist, with mitigations tracked. [Evidence]
    - In‑product transparency for key limitations and risks. [Audit]
    - Dark pattern audit across acquisition, consent, and core flows, with remediation. [Evidence]
    - Incident disclosure policy for user‑impacting events. [Audit]
- Data governance and privacy
    - Up‑to‑date data inventory, purpose, retention, and lawful basis published. [Audit]
    - Quarterly tested user data export/delete workflows with SLA tracking. [Evidence]
    - Third‑party sharing disclosures, DPAs in place, and data transfer safeguards. [Audit]
- Model transparency and safety (if AI present)
    - System or model cards covering training data sources, known risks, and evals. [Evidence]
    - Safety evals appropriate to modality and domain, with documented mitigations. [Audit]
    - Abuse monitoring and response path for model misuse. [Evidence]
- Accessibility and inclusion
    - WCAG 2.2 AA on core flows or a time‑bound remediation plan with owners. [Evidence]
    - Representative research or continuous feedback loop for impacted groups. [Self‑attest]
- Company practices and governance
    - Responsible tech policy with accountable owners and escalation paths. [Self‑attest]
    - Annual independent review against this rubric plus corrective action tracking. [Audit]

### Scoring v1.0

- Required controls: must pass all “Audit” and “Evidence” required items.
- Points: electives worth 1 point each; outcome metrics can earn bonus points.
- Levels:
    - Bronze: Required controls + 4 elective points
    - Silver: Required controls + 8 elective points, no critical findings open
    - Gold: Required controls + 12 elective points, continuous monitoring in place, time‑to‑remediation P95 ≤ 30 days

### Outcome signals (optional but recommended)

- User‑reported harm rate trends and time‑to‑mitigation
- DSR SLA performance (export/delete)
- Accessibility issue backlog burn‑down

### 4) Audit model (Vanta‑style inspiration)

- Artifacts: policies, logs, tickets, eval reports, test evidence
- Continuous checks: policy freshness, incident response SLAs, DSR SLAs
- Evidence flow: intake → pre‑checklist → evidence upload → sampling → findings → remediation window → badge issuance
- Levels:
    - Recommended Humane: evidence review, no site audit
    - Certified Humane: independent audit with sampling
    - Certified Humane Plus: monitoring and annual surveillance audit

---

### 5) Business model and network effects

- Two‑sided: vendors seek certification; buyers and the public use directory and badges
- Revenue: certification fee, monitoring subscription, directory placements, grants for public‑interest criteria
- Flywheel: launch signal list → early adopters → buyer trust → more vendors → stronger standard

### Evidence request pack (v1.0 draft)

Provide these artifacts or demos. Sample 3–5 items per category if voluminous.

- Product experience and harms
    - Red‑team logs and mitigation tickets for last 2 quarters
    - Dark pattern audit report and remediation diffs
    - Incident disclosure policy and last 2 user‑impacting postmortems
- Data governance and privacy
    - Data inventory and retention schedule
    - DSR export/delete test evidence with timestamps and SLA metrics
    - List of subprocessors, DPAs, transfer safeguards
- AI transparency and safety
    - System or model cards, eval reports, jailbreak/abuse test results
    - Abuse monitoring runbook and alert samples
- Accessibility and inclusion
    - WCAG 2.2 AA audit or plan, defect list and owners
    - Evidence of representative research or feedback loop
- Governance
    - Responsible tech policy, owners, escalation paths
    - Annual independent review report and corrective actions

### Badge usage and revocation policy (v1.0 draft)

- Usage
    - Badge levels: Recommended Humane, Certified Humane, Certified Humane Plus
    - Placement: product site footer, pricing page, app listing, press kit
    - Link behavior: must link to public certificate page with scope and expiry
- Claims
    - No implication of government or safety certification
    - No modification of badge design
- Surveillance and renewal
    - Annual surveillance review for Certified and Plus
    - Immediate disclosure of material scope changes within 30 days
- Revocation
    - Triggers: critical finding not remediated in 30 days, misrepresentation, scope violation
    - Process: notice → remediation window → public revocation notice
    - Reinstatement: re‑audit of failed controls
