📍 PART 1 — Homepage Audit (certifiedhumanetechnology.ai)
1.0 Positioning & Narrative
Current issue:

Messaging assumes certification is already live.

It is not yet clear that this is a prototype, or that you want interest, signups, and co-creators.

Doesn't explain your broader initiative (BHT, HumaneBench, movement).

Recommended fix:

Add a sub-headline near the top:

A prototype standard for measuring whether AI systems support human flourishing.
Join the early access community shaping Version 1.

Also add a clear choice:

Are you a user?
Are you a builder?

This sets up your marketplace and helps you segment leads for later.

1.1 Audience Clarity

Right now, people don’t know:

Who the site is for

What they should do next

Add two audience lanes:
FOR INDIVIDUAL USERS

“Want to know which AI systems are actually humane?”

“Get early access to our directory and help shape the first humane-tech standard.”

FOR BUSINESSES / BUILDERS

“Want your AI product evaluated?”

“Join the early pilot group for HumaneBench-powered certification.”

Add clear CTAs for each.

1.2 Credibility & Trust (Very Important)

A standard or certification must feel legitimate even in prototype form.

Add social proof from your ecosystem:

MIT AHA Lab

Amazon AGI Lab collaboration

UN Secretariat training

Center for Humane Technology alignment

The growing Humane Tech Movement (your events, hackathons)

Even subtle citations lift trust dramatically.

Add "Built by" section

Built by the Building Humane Tech initiative
with a short blurb and your flame logo.

1.3 Core Message Hierarchy
Suggested hero section rewrite:

Title:
The First Humane AI Certification (Prototype)

Subtitle:
A developing standard that evaluates whether AI systems uphold eight principles of humane technology — dignity, safety, attention, agency, and more.
Now inviting early participants.

Two buttons:

I use AI systems — keep me informed

I build AI systems — join early access

1.4 Value Proposition

Right now: value is assumed, not articulated.

Add clear benefits:

For users

Know which AI systems are safe, supportive, and non-manipulative

Get transparency you can’t get anywhere else

Help shape the future of humane tech

For businesses

Benchmark your product against humane-tech standards

Identify safety and ethical blind spots

Stand out with early adoption

Earn trust with a transparent, principle-based evaluation

1.5 CTAs & Conversion Funnel

You currently have no clear conversion flow.

Add a simple funnel:

Learn what this is

Decide which side you’re on

Sign up for interest

Primary CTA should be:
“Join the Early Access List”

You can also add:

“Download the principles”

“See the evaluation rubric (prototype)”

These give substance while keeping the site prototype-friendly.
